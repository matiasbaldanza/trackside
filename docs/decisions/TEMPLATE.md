# ADR-NNNN: Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the conflict, or the question that could not be answered
by convention. Enough detail that a reader who has never seen the code understands why this was
not obvious.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the active voice.

## Alternatives considered

Each realistic option that was on the table, with the argument for and against it, and the
specific reason it was not chosen. An option dismissed without reasoning was not really
considered.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it commits us to. Include the costs — an
ADR that lists only benefits is marketing, not a record.

## Revisiting

The conditions under which this decision should be reopened.
